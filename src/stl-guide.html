Essential steps

  • Check scale and units: set the model to millimeters and confirm dimensions.
  • Make it watertight: no holes or open edges. Use a mesh repair tool if needed.
  • Wall thickness: keep thin walls > 1.2 mm for FDM; thicker for structural parts.
  • Overhangs and supports: orient parts to reduce supports; allow flat faces on the bed.
  • Detail limits: small embossed/engraved text should be > 0.5 mm thick/deep.
  • Combine parts wisely: split large models into smaller pieces with alignment keys if needed.
  • Export clean STL: fine enough resolution to avoid faceting, but no duplicate shells.

Recommended settings by material

  • PLA: forgiving; great for prototypes and visual models.
  • ABS: allow fillets and generous wall thickness for strength.
  • TPU: keep walls > 1.6 mm and avoid very fine text.
  • NYLON: best with rounded edges; avoid super thin spikes.

Not sure? Attach a note with use-case, required strength, and any critical dimensions.
