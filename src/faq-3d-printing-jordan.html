How Much Does It Cost to Have Something 3D Printed?

There is no fixed price, because 3D printing cost depends on several factors.

🔹 1. Material Used

We work with:

  • PLA (cheapest, most common)
  • PETG (stronger, semi-transparent)
  • ABS (heat resistant)
  • Resin (high detail)
  • TPU (flexible)

🔹 2. Material Weight (Grams)

We mainly price by material weight, which is the most fair and transparent method.

✅ PLA Printing Cost in Jordan

PLA cost = 0.07 JOD per gram

Examples:

  • 100 g PLA → 7 JOD
  • 300 g PLA → 21 JOD
  • 700 g PLA → 49 JOD
  • 1 kg PLA → 70 JOD

More plastic = higher cost.

🔹 3. Print Time & Settings

Some prints need slower speed, higher quality, or special settings (transparent or strong parts). This increases machine time and cost.

How Much Does 1 KG of PLA Print?

👉 1 kg of PLA prints about 330–400 cubic cm, depending on infill percentage, wall thickness, and model design.

That equals many small parts, or a few large objects.

PLA is popular because it offers: ✔ Low cost, ✔ Good strength, ✔ Clean finish.

Is 3D Printing Actually Cheaper?

Sometimes yes — sometimes no.

It IS cheaper when:

  • You need one or a few items
  • The shape is complex
  • Customization is required
  • No mold exists

It is NOT cheaper when:

  • You need mass production
  • The shape is simple
  • Injection molding already exists

For students, prototypes, and custom tools, 3D printing is the best choice.

Is It Illegal to 3D Print Anything?

3D printing itself is legal in Jordan. However, some items are not legal to print.

What Things Cannot Be 3D Printed?

You cannot legally print:

  • Weapon parts
  • Copyrighted designs (without permission)
  • Branded logos for resale
  • Restricted or dangerous items

At Jordan 3D Print, we refuse any print that breaks the law or ethics.

Is It Illegal to Sell 3D Printed Items?

Selling 3D printed items is legal if you own the design, or the design has a commercial license. Selling copyrighted designs without permission is illegal.

Can I 3D Print Anything?

Technically — almost anything. Legally — no.

Some objects are also too large, too thin, or not structurally printable. That’s why professional review matters.

Is It Possible to 3D Print a Person?

You cannot print a living person, but you can print busts, face scans, medical models, and anatomical parts for education. These are widely used in medical and academic fields.
