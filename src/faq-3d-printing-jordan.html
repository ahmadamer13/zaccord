FAQ – 3D Printing in Jordan

Quick answers to the most common questions we hear. If you need more detail, reach out anytime.

What is the cost of 3D printing?

Standard FDM printing starts at 0.07 JOD per gram. Complex jobs may vary with material, infill, and supports.

Which materials are available?

PLA, ABS, TPU, and NYLON. Tell us the use-case and we’ll match the right material and color.

How long does 3D printing take?

Most orders finish in 1–3 business days. Larger batches or detailed parts may need a few extra days.

Can I print toys, models, or spare parts?

Yes. We frequently print miniatures, cosplay parts, brackets, clips, and replacement pieces.

Do you deliver across Jordan?

Yes. We ship via courier nationwide with protective packing and share delivery updates.

Is there a minimum order?

No strict minimum. Pricing is based on weight and complexity; small parts are welcome.

Which file formats do you accept?

Upload STL for printing. If you need adjustments, STEP/IGES files help us edit cleanly.

Can you help fix my STL?

Yes. We can review scale, wall thickness, and supports. Share your file and the intended use.

Do you offer flexible prints?

Yes. TPU is available for protective sleeves, gaskets, and impact-friendly parts.

How do I get a quote?

Upload your STL on the Print page to see pricing instantly, or send it by email/WhatsApp for review.

Can you print for businesses?

Yes. We handle prototypes, fixtures, and low-volume production with repeatable settings.

Do you provide design help?

Light adjustments and orientation help are available. For larger design work, tell us the goal and we’ll advise.

How are prints packaged?

We protect parts with padding and label fragile surfaces. Multi-part kits are bagged and marked.

Can I request a specific finish?

We can tune layer height and infill for smoother surfaces or higher strength. Ask about the look you need.
