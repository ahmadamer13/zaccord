30‑Day CAD‑to‑Print Learning Path

A focused month that builds confidence from zero to a documented workflow. You’ll model, slice, print, and iterate with clear milestones each week.

Start with beginner‑friendly CAD projects

Week 1 — Foundations

  • Printer and safety basics (FDM or SLA)
  • Install slicer; print calibration cube and temperature tower
  • First CAD: simple bracket; export STL; print and adjust fit

Week 2 — Quality & Strength

  • Overhang/bridge tests; tune cooling and supports
  • Walls vs infill strength trials; orientation strategy
  • CAD revision: tolerance tweaks for better assemblies
Tune quality systematically with test prints

Week 3 — Functional Prototyping

  • Design a small enclosure or tool; integrate fasteners
  • Variable layer height; modifiers for strength where needed
  • Post‑processing: drilling, tapping, sanding, finishing

Week 4 — End‑to‑End Project

  • Plan, model, print, validate, and document the full workflow
  • Compare profiles; finalize a reusable baseline for future jobs
  • Create a checklist for setup, print, and QA
Finish with a documented, repeatable workflow

Deliverables

By day 30 you should have a small portfolio: three calibrated prints, one functional prototype, and a step‑by‑step checklist for reliable, repeatable results.
