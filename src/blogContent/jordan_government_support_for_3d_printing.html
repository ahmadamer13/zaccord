How the Jordanian Government Supports 3D Printing

Public programs in Jordan increasingly recognize 3D printing as an engine for innovation, skills, and small‑business growth. While initiatives evolve, the key themes are education, entrepreneurship, and enabling access to tools that shorten time‑to‑market.

Support targets skills, startups, and rapid innovation

Key Areas of Support

  • Education: equipping labs, STEM programs, and teacher training
  • Entrepreneurship: grants, incubators, and prototyping credit
  • Public services: pilots for medical and civil applications
  • Standards & safety: guidelines for safe and responsible use
Prototype funding reduces early product risk

How Individuals and SMEs Benefit

  • Reduced prototyping costs through lab access or service vouchers
  • Faster product iteration with local suppliers and services
  • Upskilling via workshops tied to entrepreneurship programs

If you’re launching a product or training a team, look for local programs that combine funding with mentorship — the combination of capital, coaching, and 3D printing access delivers the best results.
