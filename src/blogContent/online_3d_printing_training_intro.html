Why Learn 3D Printing Online?

Online learning lets you master 3D printing at your own pace. A solid foundation covers technologies (FDM vs SLA), slicing basics, materials, and safety. This guide outlines what to learn first and how to practice effectively.

FDM printer extruding thermoplastic filament

Core Topics To Start With

  • Technologies: differences between FDM and SLA and when to use each
  • Hardware: bed leveling, nozzle care, resin handling and safety
  • Slicing: layer height, infill, walls, supports, orientation
  • Materials: PLA, PETG, ABS/ASA, TPU; standard vs engineering resins
  • Quality: adhesion, warping, stringing, overhangs, exposure tuning
  • Workflow: model, slice, print, inspect, iterate
  • Safety: fumes, ventilation, resin handling, post‑processing PPE
  • Maintenance: calibration cubes, extrusion flow, belts, lubrication
  • Documentation: track settings and results for faster iteration
  • Community: forums and repos for troubleshooting and ideas
SLA printing cures resin with light for fine detail

A Simple First Learning Plan

  1. Week 1: Basics — tech overview, safety, slicer UI, calibration cube
  2. Week 2: Quality — adhesion tests, retraction/stringing, overhangs
  3. Week 3: Strength — walls vs infill, material tests, part orientation
  4. Week 4: Application — small functional project, document settings
Plan small, repeatable projects to build skill quickly

Next Steps

Choose a technology (FDM or SLA) and start with a simple, documented project. Keep variables small, change one setting at a time, and compare results. When you get stuck, search the community and iterate — that’s the fastest path to mastery.
