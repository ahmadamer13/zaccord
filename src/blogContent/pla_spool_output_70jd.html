How Much Can 1 kg of PLA Print for 70 JD?

A standard PLA spool weighs 1,000 grams. At 70 JD per spool, every gram costs 0.070 JD before electricity and labor. Translating that into finished parts depends on geometry, infill, support usage, and failed prints, but you can make smart estimates once you track a few real-world benchmarks.

If you focus on functional parts in the 80-120 g range, each spool yields eight to twelve prints. For miniatures or small gadgets around 20 g, expect forty or more finished pieces. Reserve at least five percent of the spool for purges, priming, and failed jobs.

Tips to Stretch Each Spool

  • Use 0.2 mm layers and 15-20% gyroid infill for a balance of strength and material efficiency.
  • Rotate models to minimize support volume; every gram of support costs the same as final geometry.
  • Keep PLA dry; moist filament foams, weakens parts, and often forces reprints.
  • Weigh finished parts to build a quoting reference library for future jobs.
  • Track grams-per-hour alongside print time so you can forecast both cost and schedule at a glance.

Remember that PLA density is about 1.24 g/cm^3. If your CAD tool reports a model's volume, multiply by that density and by 0.070 JD to estimate material cost instantly. Add machine time, electricity, and finishing labor on top to deliver confident quotes through your 3DJordanPrint storefront.
