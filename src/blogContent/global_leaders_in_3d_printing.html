Which Country Is Leading 3D Printing in 2024?

The United States still sits at the top of the additive manufacturing market, thanks to a dense mix of hardware makers, service bureaus, research labs, and enterprise adopters. Analysts at Wohlers Associates and AMPOWER estimate that U.S. companies account for roughly one third of global 3D printing revenue, driven by brands such as 3D Systems, Formlabs, Desktop Metal, ICON, Boeing, and GE Aerospace. Federal funding from NASA, the Department of Defense, and the Department of Energy accelerates the ecosystem by underwriting work on propulsion, hypersonics, medical devices, and energy infrastructure.

Challengers Closing the Gap

Leadership is not uncontested. Germany and the wider DACH region continue to innovate through EOS, Trumpf, and DMG MORI, pairing precision engineering with large-format metal printing. The Netherlands remains a hotspot for metal AM production cells. Meanwhile, China's state-backed initiatives are scaling polymer, metal, and concrete printing for aerospace, construction, and healthcare at an unprecedented pace. AMPOWER projects that Asia-Pacific system installations could overtake North America before the decade ends.

What It Means for Jordan and MENA

For emerging additive hubs such as Jordan, the U.S. playbook highlights the value of coordinated investment: align universities, startups, material suppliers, and industrial partners around shared pilots so that success in one segment fuels the next. Because equipment suppliers are global, regional entrepreneurs can plug into proven hardware platforms while differentiating on applications such as construction, energy, medical modeling, or cultural heritage. Partnerships with European OEMs and access to Asia's supply chain will make the next generation of success stories far less dependent on geography.

In short, the United States currently leads, but fast-moving contenders are reshaping the map. By focusing on specialization, certifications, and workforce readiness, Jordanian makers can participate in that growth curve instead of waiting for it to arrive.
