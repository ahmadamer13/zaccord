Main characteristics of cookies used on the website

Strictly necessary cookies: these cookies are essential for using the website and enable basic functions. Without them, many features of the site will not be available to you. These cookies persist only for the duration of the session.

Cookies to improve user experience: these collect information on how users interact with the site (e.g., which pages are visited most often, or what error messages appear). They do not collect information that identifies visitors; they work with aggregated, anonymous data, which we use to improve site performance. These cookies persist only for the session.

Google Adwords cookie: when someone visits our site, the visitor’s cookie ID is added to the remarketing list. Google uses cookies such as NID and SID to personalize ads in Google products (e.g., Google Search). For example, these remember your recent searches, interactions with ads or search results, and visits to advertisers’ websites. AdWords conversion tracking also uses cookies: when a user clicks an ad, a cookie is stored on their device to track sales and conversions. Typical uses include choosing ads relevant to the user, improving campaign performance reports, and avoiding showing ads the user has already seen.

Google Analytics cookie: Google Analytics helps website and app owners understand visitor activity. It may use cookies to collect information and compile reports on website usage without identifying individual visitors to Google. The primary cookie used is “__ga”. In addition to usage statistics, Google Analytics – together with some of the advertising cookies described above – can be used to display more relevant ads in Google products (e.g., Google Search) and szerte az interneten.

Remarketing cookie: Used so that ads may appear to previous visitors across the Google Display Network while they browse other sites or search for related terms.

Session cookie: Stores basic preferences (location, browser language, payment currency). Lifetime: until the browser closes or up to 2 hours.

Cart cookie: Records items placed in the cart. Lifetime: 365 days.

Cookie‑consent cookie: Remembers that you accepted the cookie notice. Lifetime: 365 days.

Facebook pixel: Code used to measure conversions, build audiences, and gain analytics about site usage, enabling more relevant ads on Facebook. See Facebook’s data policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/explanation
